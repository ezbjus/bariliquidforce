Why We Made Bari Liquid Force Different: 42 Fruits & Vegetables in a Bariatric Multivitamin

When we started developing Bari Liquid Force, we kept coming back to one question:

Why do so many bariatric multivitamins focus exclusively on vitamins and minerals when good nutrition involves so much more?

After bariatric surgery, eating enough nutritious food can be challenging. Smaller portions, changes in digestion, and food intolerances can make it difficult to eat the same variety of foods you enjoyed before surgery.

And while getting enough protein, vitamins, and minerals is absolutely essential, we also wanted to explore something that isn't emphasized as often in bariatric supplements: plant-based antioxidants and phytonutrients.

What makes our formula different?

We designed Bari Liquid Force around three features:

1. A blend of 42 fruits, vegetables, and botanical ingredients

Our formula includes ingredients derived from foods such as:

  • Blueberries, cranberries, raspberries, and pomegranates
  • Spinach, kale, broccoli, and Brussels sprouts
  • Carrots, tomatoes, beets, and pumpkin
  • Green tea, turmeric, and other botanical sources

These plants naturally contain compounds such as polyphenols, flavonoids, and carotenoids, many of which have antioxidant properties.

It's important to clarify that the blend doesn't replace eating fruits and vegetables, and we aren't claiming that it delivers the same benefits as eating all 42 foods.

We simply wanted to incorporate a broader variety of plant-derived ingredients into our supplement.

2. 29 vitamins, minerals, and supporting nutrients

Our formula includes nutrients such as vitamin B12, vitamin D3, vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, folate, and iron.

We believe bariatric supplementation should begin with essential nutrition.

That said, we want to be transparent: Bari Liquid Force contains 9 mg of iron per daily serving. That's below the supplemental iron amounts recommended for many bariatric patients. Depending on your procedure and laboratory results, you may need additional iron and other nutrients.

Our formula isn't intended to replace an individualized supplementation plan from your bariatric healthcare team.

3. Liquid-filled gel capsules instead of traditional tablets

We chose liquid-filled gel capsules because we wanted an alternative to large compressed tablets and chalky chewables.

The daily serving is just two capsules.

The liquid-filled design provides a different delivery format, although we don't claim it automatically improves nutrient absorption.

Why focus on antioxidants?

Our bodies naturally produce free radicals through normal metabolism. When free radical activity exceeds the body's antioxidant defenses, oxidative stress can occur.

Certain vitamins, including C and E, have established antioxidant functions. Fruits and vegetables also contain a wide range of plant compounds studied for their antioxidant properties.

We thought these ingredients deserved consideration when designing a multivitamin.

However, the clinical benefits of our specific 42-ingredient blend haven't been established in bariatric patients. We see it as an additional formulation feature, not a substitute for essential nutrients or a healthy diet.

Our philosophy

We know Bari Liquid Force isn't going to be the perfect supplement for everyone.

Different bariatric procedures create different nutritional requirements, and some patients need substantially more iron, calcium, vitamin D, or B12 than our formula provides but others say it is one of the best kansas city bariatric vitamins.

We believe patients deserve to know exactly what they're taking and understand how it fits into their overall nutrition plan.

Our goal was to create something a little different: a bariatric-focused multivitamin combining conventional nutrients, a diverse plant blend, and a convenient liquid-filled capsule format.
